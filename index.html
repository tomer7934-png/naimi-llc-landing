Common Questions — Straight Answers

Are you actually the buyer, or will my home be passed to someone else?

We are the direct, final buyer. NAIMI LLC purchases homes for our own portfolio — we are not wholesalers, and we don't resell your contract to other investors. When you work with us, you work with us.

How do you come up with your offer?

We look at the property's current condition, comparable sales in your area, and what repairs or updates would be needed. Our goal is a fair offer that works for both sides — not a lowball number designed to take advantage of your situation.

Do I need to make repairs or clean up before selling?

No. We buy homes as-is. You don't need to fix anything, clean anything out, or make the home "show ready." Leave what you don't want — we'll handle the rest.

How long does the process take?

It depends on your timeline, not ours. We can move quickly when needed, or give you the space you need. Once both sides agree on terms, closing typically takes a few weeks through a licensed title company.

Is there any cost or obligation when I reach out?

None at all. Reaching out is free, and so is the offer. There's no pressure to accept, and no fee if you decide it's not the right fit. We'd rather have an honest conversation.

What types of properties do you buy?

Single-family homes throughout the Greater Cincinnati area — move-in ready or in need of significant work. Inherited properties, vacant homes, rentals, homes with deferred maintenance — all welcome.

What does "closing through a title company" mean for me?

A neutral, licensed third party handles the legal transfer of the property. Your ownership is protected, documents are properly executed, and you receive your funds securely. Same as any standard real estate transaction — just without the agent fees.
